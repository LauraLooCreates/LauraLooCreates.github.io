Music

Violin and music is one of my truest loves. It began during Covid when I would play out on the balcony. It is here I learned the magic of improvisation. From there I spent hours after school making up my own songs and learning the whole concept of music theory. I took my love (and newly-made songs) to the streets and ever since then I have been given the opportunity to play at multiple events, improvising as I go.
